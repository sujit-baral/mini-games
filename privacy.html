Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026. Plain English, transparent, and tracker-free.

1. No Personal Data Collection

Mini Game Arcade is dedicated to user privacy. We do not require account registration, do not collect personal names, emails, physical locations, or payment credentials, and do not track individual users across the web.

2. Browser LocalStorage Usage

To provide persistent gameplay features without an external database, our mini-games use standard browser localStorage exclusively for:

  • Saving your personal highest scores for each game mode.
  • Saving your audio mute/unmute preferences so the sound remains as you left it.
  • Tracking whether the first-time tutorial overlay has been dismissed.

This data resides entirely on your device and is never uploaded, shared, or transmitted to any server. You can wipe this data at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

3. No Third-Party Tracking or Advertising Cookies

We do not use advertising networks, behavioral tracking scripts, social media tracking pixels, or cross-site fingerprinting cookies.

4. Static Hosting

This static website is deployed via Vercel's global Content Delivery Network (CDN). Standard server access logs (such as IP addresses and user agents) may be temporarily processed by the hosting provider strictly for security diagnostics and DDoS prevention in compliance with standard network protocols.

5. Contacting Us

If you have questions about our privacy commitments, please reach out via our Contact page.
