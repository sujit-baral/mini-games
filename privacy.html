Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026. Transparent, compliant, and privacy-conscious.

1. Introduction & Ownership

Mini Game Arcade is an independent open-web browser gaming platform. We believe high-quality gaming should not come at the expense of your privacy. This policy outlines how information is handled when you access and play games on our site.

2. No Personal Data Collection

We do not require account registration, passwords, or personal profiles. We do not collect names, telephone numbers, home addresses, payment information, or precise geolocation. You can play every game anonymously.

3. Browser Local Storage Usage

To provide persistent gameplay features without relying on external databases or tracking accounts, our games utilize your browser's client-side localStorage solely for:

  • Saving your personal high scores and best completion times.
  • Remembering your audio mute/unmute preferences between sessions.
  • Tracking whether introductory game instructions have been viewed.

This data remains stored exclusively on your device. It is never uploaded, sold, or shared with our servers or any third parties. You can clear this data at any time through your browser's history or site settings.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Cookie Disclosures

Current Status: Mini Game Arcade does not currently place third-party advertising cookies or behavioral tracking scripts on your device.

Advertising Partner Policies (Google AdSense Readiness): In accordance with Google AdSense program policies, please note the following terms that govern third-party advertising:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites across the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by visiting www.aboutads.info.

5. Static Hosting & Infrastructure Logs

This website is hosted statically via Vercel's global Content Delivery Network (CDN). Like virtually all web servers, standard non-identifying server logs (such as IP address, browser user-agent, and requested file path) may be temporarily processed by the hosting infrastructure strictly for cybersecurity, DDoS mitigation, and network routing in compliance with global data protection standards.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our games are casual, general-audience puzzle and reflex games. We do not intentionally target, collect, or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

7. External Links

Our website may contain links to external resources (such as GitHub or documentation). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party external sites.
