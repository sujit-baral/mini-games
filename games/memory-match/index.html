🎮 How to Play & Controls

🖱️ Mouse & Touch

Click or tap any two hidden cards to flip them. If they match, they lock in place. If not, they flip back over.

⌨️ Keyboard Navigation

Use [Arrow Keys] to navigate the matrix of cards. Press [Space] or [Enter] to flip the focused card.

⚡ Pro Memory Strategy

Keep track of symbol positions you reveal. Fewer moves give higher accuracy and better arcade rankings!

🧠 Difficulty Tiers

Choose your training mode to sharpen recall speed and spatial memory:

  • Easy Mode: 12 cards (6 pairs) • Great for beginners & quick reflex warm-ups
  • Classic Mode: 16 cards (8 pairs) • Balanced standard arcade memory challenge
  • Expert Mode: 20 cards (10 pairs) • Maximum concentration grid for master gamers
