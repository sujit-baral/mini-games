How to Play Doodle Guess Arcade

Doodle Guess Arcade is a fast-paced multiplayer drawing and word-guessing game built exclusively with modern open web platform primitives (HTML5 Canvas 2D, Web Audio API, and WebRTC Peer-to-Peer data channels). Connect with friends anywhere across mobile and desktop with zero account registrations or software downloads!

✏️ The Drawer

When it is your turn, choose 1 of 3 secret words. Draw visual clues on the canvas without writing letters, numbers, or spelling the word out. The faster your friends guess, the more bonus points you earn!

🔍 The Guessers

Watch the canvas closely as strokes appear in real-time. Type your answers into the guess chat. Letter hints reveal themselves as the round clock counts down. The first player to guess correctly gets the top score!

🌐 Peer-to-Peer Networking

Multiplayer matches run directly browser-to-browser via WebRTC data channels. Your drawing coordinates stream with sub-50ms latency without uploading any sketches to third-party databases.

Scoring Breakdown

  • Guesser Score: 100 base points + (Remaining seconds × 5). Early guesses earn up to 400 points!
  • Drawer Bonus: +50 points for each player in the room who successfully guesses your drawing.
  • Solo Bot Mode: Test your skills solo! CyberBot draws procedural sketches while you race the 45-second clock.

Game Rules & Tips

👥 Multiplayer: One player clicks "Host Room" and shares the room link. Friends join from their phone or computer.

✏️ Drawing: Select colors and brush sizes from the toolbar. Draw smoothly with mouse or touch. No letters or spelling allowed!

💬 Guessing: Guessers type answers into the chat box. Correct guesses award points based on how fast you answer.

🤖 Solo Mode: Don't have friends online right now? Play the Solo Bot Challenge to guess animated sketches drawn by CyberBot!
